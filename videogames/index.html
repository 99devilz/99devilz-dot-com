As of 2025, I've started making video games seriously (not like. boring serious but like in its now my main thing that I do as my job type serious)

The projects I'm mostly working on these days are Dungeon Pizza and CyberBuglets, but I still like to make time for a jam or two.

o

Dungeon Pizza

"A cooking game with a fantasy twist! Knead the dough, spread the sauce, and prepare… the rat chunks?! Open your own pizzeria deep inside a dungeon, serving weary adventurers who come to rest and refuel between their quests and bounties."

DP has been in progress since February 2025 and was a project born out of the Screen QLD Games Residency. We are a 5 person team, I mostly contribute 2D art assets and animations.

Wishlist on steam!!

Cyber Buglets

"Years after its closure, a popular early-2000s social MMO unexpectedly flickers back to life. Cyber Buglets has returned, and it invites players to log in again. "

Cyber Buglets was originally going to be a micro-game, but we loved the idea so much we wanted to make it into a whole game. my hart

Wishlist on steam!!

On the Hearth

"As an apprentice wise-woman during the height of the witch trials, decrypt decades of magical research, explore the wilds for peculiar reagents, and experiment with arcane potions. All the while solving a myriad of mysteries whilst preparing your close-knit village for an encroaching church-crusade."

I worked on OTH alot at the start of its development, now I just drop in every now and again to do some character designs.

Wishlist on steam!!

Kaiju Critters

"Kaiju Critters is a Turn-based Tactics game where the giant bugs flip the script and save the day against an invasion of giant robots. Protect Brisbane by playing skill-based micro games while crawling, skittering, and climbing to out manoeuvre your enemies in a free form 3D environment."

I worked the 2D stuff!

Pupiils

"Med students Sloane and Eustace complete a research survey on residents with a mysterious chronic skin condition."

Most recent jam as of writing, made for the Weird RPG Jam Oscarno hosted. LOVED working on this, I really want to return to RPGMaker for fun side projects.

Find it on Itch.io

Dead Ears

"The cornfield stretches on forever.
A light stands blinking atop a distant tower.
A voice crackles over the radio...
DEAD EARS is a single-player horror exploration game where players use a radio to scare crows. "

This was a fun 1 week jam, I was on 2D assets.

Find it on Itch.io

Blood City Limits

"Mabel awakens to a broken, pulsating shadow of a world. Alone. A feeling in her gut knows she needs to find something. Something, or someone. Under the blood red sky, she begins her search for answers. As she walks the cracked streets and twisted paths, she can only hope to find someone to go through this with. No one wants to go through the end of the world alone."

Month long game jam (NanoReno2024) and one of the first decently sized game projects I was a part of. I made the monsters and backgrounds.
